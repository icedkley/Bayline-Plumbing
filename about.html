What we do

Bayline Plumbing handles residential and commercial plumbing across Corpus Christi and the surrounding communities. That includes emergency calls, drain cleaning, water heaters, leak detection, sewer line repair, fixture installation, and the everyday repairs that don't fit a category.

We work with homeowners, landlords, property managers, and small business owners. The scope is the same whether it's a house on the island or a commercial building off SPID — diagnose the problem, explain what we find, and quote the fix before we start.

How we approach the work

Straightforward service

We tell you what we find and what we recommend, in plain language. No pressure, no upsell, no jargon. If something can wait, we'll say so. If it needs to be handled now, we'll explain why.

Clear communication

You'll know what to expect and when. If something changes during the job — a part that's seized, a surprise under the sink — we stop and let you know before we keep going. No surprise charges after the fact.

Where we work

We're based in Corpus Christi and serve the surrounding Coastal Bend. Most of our work is in Corpus Christi proper, Portland, Robstown, and Aransas Pass, plus the communities in between. If you're not sure whether we cover your area, ask when you reach out.
