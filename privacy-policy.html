Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy describes how Bayline Plumbing handles information submitted through this website. This is a demonstration build, not a live business.

Information we collect

When you submit the contact form, we collect the information you enter: name, business or property name (optional), email, phone, and a short message about what you need. We don't collect anything else automatically — no tracking scripts, no analytics, no advertising cookies.

How we use it

The information you submit is used only to respond to your request. In a live version of this site, that would mean a team member reaching out to discuss your project. It wouldn't be sold, shared, or used for marketing.

What this demo actually does

This is a demonstration website. The contact form doesn't submit anywhere — no message is sent, no data is stored, and no one receives your information. When you submit the form, the page simply shows a success message. You can safely test the form with any information you like.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set cookies and doesn't use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising scripts. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Third-party services

None. The site loads no external fonts, no CDN assets, no CDN-hosted libraries, and no third-party JavaScript. All CSS, JavaScript, and images are served from this domain.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at hello@baylineplumbing.example. In a live version of the site, that would be a monitored inbox.
