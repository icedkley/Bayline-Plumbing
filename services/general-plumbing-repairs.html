What we work on

Everyday plumbing problems that don't fit a bigger category. If it's not listed, ask — we probably handle it.

Running or leaking toilets

Flapper, fill valve, or seal failure — usually a quick fix that stops the water bill from climbing.

Dripping faucets and valves

Worn cartridges, seats, or O-rings. Often fixable without replacing the whole fixture.

Low water pressure

Aerator clog, failing valve, or a supply line issue — we find the cause, not just the symptom.

Slow drains in one fixture

Kitchen or bath drain that isn't flowing right but isn't fully clogged — often buildup in the trap or branch line.

Noisy pipes

Banging, whistling, or humming when water runs — loose pipe, water hammer, or a worn valve.

Small leaks and weeping joints

Anywhere water shows up that isn't coming from a burst pipe — under a sink, at a valve, at a supply connection.

How we handle a repair

Two things matter: knowing what's wrong, and doing only the work that's needed.

Diagnose it

We ask what you're seeing and hearing, then look at the likely cause. Most everyday plumbing issues fall into a small number of categories — a worn part, a partial clog, a loose connection, or a pressure issue. Diagnosis is usually quick, and we tell you what we find.

Get an estimate

Fix it

Repair depends on what we find. Sometimes it's a small part — a cartridge, a flapper, a washer. Sometimes it's a section of pipe or a valve that's reached the end of its life. We quote the repair before we start, and we don't replace parts that don't need replacing.

Get an estimate

What it costs

Everyday plumbing repairs are priced by the job, not the hour. A running toilet and a dripping shower valve are different jobs with different parts, and we quote them that way.

Estimates are free. If a repair is straightforward, we'll quote it before we start. If we find something bigger — a failing valve, a corroded section, a hidden leak — we'll tell you before we touch it, and you decide how to proceed.

Most general repairs run $150–$400. Larger repairs or multi-fixture jobs run more — we'll quote before we start.
