Fixture Installation in Corpus Christi

Replacing a faucet, swapping out a toilet, or updating a sink? We handle removal and installation cleanly — including the small stuff that causes leaks.

What we install and replace

Residential and commercial fixtures, big and small. If it connects to the water or drain, we can install it.

Kitchen and bathroom faucets

Swap an old fixture, upgrade to a pull-down, or replace a failing cartridge — including supply lines and shut-off valves.

Toilets and bidets

Full removal and installation — new wax ring, new bolts, new supply line. We'll take the old one away if you want.

Sinks and basins

Kitchen, bathroom, utility, and pedestal sinks. We fit the drain, the trap, and the supply lines.

Shower and tub valves

Replace a worn mixer, upgrade to a pressure-balance or thermostatic valve, or install a hand-shower.

Garbage disposals

New install or replacement — mount, drain connection, and dishwasher hookup if applicable.

Outdoor hose bibs and ice maker lines

Install or replace freeze-resistant hose bibs and route ice maker supply lines cleanly.

What we handle on every install

Two things matter: a clean installation, and no leaks afterward.

What we handle

We remove the old fixture, prep the surface or opening, and install the new one with fresh supply lines, shut-off valves, and seals. If the wall or cabinet needs a small adjustment to fit the new fixture, we tell you before we start.

Get an estimate

What we check

Before we call the job done, we check for leaks at every connection, verify the fixture works the way it should, and make sure nothing drips or weeps overnight. If a shut-off valve is seized or the drain is corroded, we flag it and quote the fix — you decide whether to do it now or later.

Get an estimate

What it costs

Fixture installation is priced by the fixture and how much of the surrounding plumbing needs updating. A like-for-like faucet swap is a quick job. A full toilet replacement with new supply and shut-off is a bit more.

Estimates are free. If we find something unexpected — a seized valve, a cracked flange, a drain that won't come loose — we'll quote it before we touch it. No surprise charges after the fact.

Most fixture installations run $150–$500 depending on the fixture and complexity. Full shower valve replacements and multi-fixture jobs run more — we'll quote before we start.
